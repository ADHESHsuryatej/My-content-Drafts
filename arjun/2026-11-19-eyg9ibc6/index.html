The top 5 customer journey orchestration tools in 2026

Customer journey orchestration tools route events through delays, branches and retries so a single trigger produces the right message on the right channel at the right time. We compared five platforms on workflow depth for product notifications: visual builders, node types, versioning, multi-tenancy and pricing.

The short answer. Choose Courier if you want a visual journey builder with per-node delivery windows and an MCP server for AI-assisted authoring. Choose SuprSend if you need batch, digest, throttle and up to 10 branch paths plus smart channel routing and per-tenant vendor fallback. Choose Knock if built-in multi-tenancy on every plan and a guide system for in-product announcements matter most. Choose Novu if you prefer open-source core with workflow-run billing and a self-hosted option. Choose Customer.io if send-time optimization and per-channel frequency limits are table stakes.

  • Courier: Best for: Visual journeys with per-node timing controls and MCP authoring; Starting price: Free tier (10k sends/mo)
  • SuprSend: Best for: Deep workflow nodes, smart routing, multi-tenant vendor fallback; Starting price: Free tier (10k notifications/mo)
  • Knock: Best for: Built-in multi-tenancy, guides for in-product UI; Starting price: Free tier (10k messages/mo)
  • Novu: Best for: Open-source core, workflow-run billing, self-hosted; Starting price: Free tier (10k workflow runs/mo)
  • Customer.io: Best for: Send-time optimization, per-channel frequency limits; Starting price: $100/mo Essentials

Courier

Courier is a notification platform whose Journeys product gives you a drag-and-drop canvas for building cross-channel sequences. Each node can set its own delivery window or quiet hours, so a security alert sends immediately while a promotional push waits for the user's morning. Send rate limits on any provider queue excess traffic instead of dropping it. The hosted MCP server lets you author, test and debug workflows from chat clients such as Claude Code or VS Code Copilot. A multi-channel template counts as one send regardless of how many channels deliver.

Pros: Per-node delivery windows; hosted MCP server for AI-assisted authoring. Cons: No built-in batch or digest nodes; multi-tenancy requires Enterprise. Pricing: Developer free (10k sends/mo), Business $0.005/send, Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

SuprSend

SuprSend is a notification infrastructure layer that sits above your email, SMS, WhatsApp and push providers. Its workflow engine includes batch, digest (fixed or user-chosen schedule), throttle and if/else-if/else branching up to 10 paths on payload, user, tenant or earlier message status. Smart channel routing tries one channel at a time with delays and stops once the user engages. Versioning (draft/live, rollback), idempotency keys and per-tenant vendor fallback are built in. Multi-tenancy with sub-tenants up to five levels and per-tenant vendors is gated to Enterprise or a paid add-on.

Pros: Batch, digest, throttle and 10-path branching; smart channel routing and per-tenant vendor fallback. Cons: Batching, digest, preferences and objects need Business ($250/mo billed annually); multi-tenancy is Enterprise or add-on. Pricing: Free (10k notifications/mo), Essentials $100/mo billed annually (50k), Business $250/mo billed annually (50k), Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Knock

Knock provides a workflow engine with batch, delay and branching nodes plus a Guides system for in-product announcements such as upgrade modals or outage banners. Built-in multi-tenancy is available on every plan, including the free tier. Guides pricing uses monthly engaged users (2.5k on Starter) rather than message volume. The platform also offers email client previews and SSO via Google on the free plan.

Pros: Built-in multi-tenancy on all plans; Guides for in-product UI with native React/Vue/Angular components. Cons: Guide active-user pricing adds a second meter; translation (i18n) and per-customer branding are Enterprise-only. Pricing: Developer free (10k messages/mo), Starter $250/mo (50k messages), Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Novu

Novu is an open-source notification infrastructure with a managed cloud tier. Cloud billing is based on workflow runs (one execution for one subscriber) rather than messages or channels. Free and Pro plans cap workflows at 20, steps per workflow at 20, and digest/delay/throttle durations at 1 day/7 days/1 hour respectively; Team raises those to 100 workflows, 30-day digests and 7-day throttles. The open-source project can be self-hosted at no cost from Novu.

Pros: Open-source core with self-hosted option; workflow-run billing is predictable for fan-out scenarios. Cons: Low step and workflow caps on Free/Pro; digest and throttle durations capped by plan; custom limits may incur extra cost. Pricing: Free (10k workflow runs/mo), Pro $30/mo (30k+), Team $250/mo (250k+), Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Customer.io

Customer.io combines a visual workflow builder with an AI Agent (beta) that can execute core and custom skills inside journeys. Message frequency limits apply per channel (email, push, SMS, WhatsApp, LINE, webhooks) and can be scoped to specific workflows or messages. Send-time optimization is included on all paid plans. The platform also supports unlimited push, in-app and webhook volume on Essentials.

Pros: Send-time optimization included; per-channel frequency limits with workflow-level scoping; AI Agent with execution skills. Cons: Essentials starts at $100/mo billed monthly; AI Agent daily routines gated to Premium ($1,000/mo); SMS volume is custom-priced. Pricing: Essentials from $100/mo, Premium from $1,000/mo, Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Each tool solves the same problem (turning an event into a reliable, timed, multi-channel notification) but the levers they expose differ. Pick the one whose workflow depth matches the complexity you actually ship.
