The top 6 SendGrid alternatives in 2026

Transactional email APIs power password resets, order confirmations and security alerts. When SendGrid's pricing jumps, deliverability dips or you need multichannel workflows, you start looking for a SendGrid alternative. We compared six providers on deliverability tooling, pricing transparency, developer experience and multi-channel support.

The short answer

Choose Mailgun if you want a full deliverability suite and EU/US sending regions. Choose Postmark if transactional speed, message streams and template workflows are your priority. Choose Amazon SES for the lowest raw cost and AWS integration. Choose MailerSend if you need SMS and WhatsApp on the same platform. Choose Brevo for a free tier with multichannel marketing automation. Choose SuprSend when you need one orchestration layer that routes across any provider you already pay for.

Mailgun

Mailgun is an email API and SMTP service with a built-in deliverability suite (inbox placement testing, blocklist monitoring, Google Postmaster and Microsoft SNDS integration) and data centers in both the US and EU. It suits teams that want deliverability tooling on the same platform they send from. Blocklist monitoring checks sender IPs against known blocklists. Google Postmaster Tools provides domain reputation data. Microsoft SNDS offers similar insights for Outlook/Hotmail.

Pros: Deliverability suite included on paid plans; EU and US sending regions. Cons: Free tier limited to 100 emails/day; dedicated IPs start at $59/month. Pricing: Free plan (100 emails/day). Paid plans start at $15/month for 10,000 emails; dedicated IPs from $59/month.

Postmark

Postmark focuses exclusively on transactional email with message streams that separate transactional, bulk and broadcast traffic, a template workflow optimised for operational email and configurable retention up to 365 days. It fits teams that treat transactional email as a reliability concern.

Pros: Message streams isolate traffic types; template-first sending path; 45-day default retention. Cons: No marketing features; 50-recipient limit per API call. Pricing: Free Developer plan (100 emails/month). Basic $15/month for 10,000 emails ($1.80/1k overage). Pro $16.50/month ($1.30/1k).

Amazon SES

Amazon SES introduced three hierarchical plans (Essentials, Pro, Enterprise) where Essentials provides deliverability insights, Pro adds managed dedicated IPs, email validation, and global inbox placement visibility, and Enterprise adds multi-region resilience. New accounts start in a sandbox limited to verified recipients and daily volume caps until a limit increase is approved. The AWS Free Tier includes 3,000 emails/month for 12 months. Virtual Deliverability Manager automates recommendations.

Pros: Pay-as-you-go base rates; Virtual Deliverability Manager automates recommendations Cons: Sandbox restricts new accounts; deliverability tooling gated behind Pro/Enterprise plans Pricing: AWS Free Tier includes 3,000 emails/month for 12 months. Essentials/Pro/Enterprise plan pricing is not public; base sending remains per 1,000 emails

MailerSend

MailerSend combines email API, SMTP relay, SMS (US/CA) and WhatsApp APIs with a drag-and-drop builder, DMARC/blocklist monitoring and an MCP server for AI-assisted workflows It targets startups and agencies that want multichannel transactional messaging on one bill.

Pros: SMS and WhatsApp on paid tiers; MCP server access; generous domain/template limits Cons: Free plan pauses at 500 emails/month; Hobby plan caps at 5,000 emails/month. Pricing: Free (500 emails/month). Hobby $5.60/month billed yearly (5,000 emails). Starter/Professional from $25/month (50,000 emails, $1.30/1k overage)

Brevo (Sendinblue)

Brevo is an all-in-one platform covering email, SMS, WhatsApp, chat and marketing automation with a free tier of 300 daily emails and up to 100,000 contacts Batch sending supports 1,000 message versions per API call at 6,000 calls/hour.

Pros: Free tier includes multichannel automation; batch API up to 6M versions/hour Cons: Daily send cap on Free plan; dedicated IPs and SSO require Professional/Enterprise. Pricing: Free (300 emails/day). Starter from $9/month (tiers by volume/contacts). Standard from $18/month. Professional from $499/month. Enterprise custom.

SuprSend

SuprSend is a notification orchestration layer: you bring your own email, SMS, WhatsApp, push and in-app provider accounts, and SuprSend handles workflows, batching, digests, preferences and multi-tenant routing from a single dashboard, API or AI agent. It fits teams that want channel-agnostic logic without migrating providers.

Pros: Provider-agnostic routing with fallback; deep multi-tenant model (sub-tenants, per-tenant vendors); in-app inbox and preference centre included. Cons: Batching, digests and preferences require Business plan ($250/mo billed annually); multi-tenancy is Enterprise or paid add-on; not open source. Pricing: Free (10,000 notifications/month). Essentials $100/mo billed annually (50k). Business $250/mo billed annually (50k, adds batching/digest/preferences/objects). Enterprise custom [https://www.suprsend.com/pricing].
