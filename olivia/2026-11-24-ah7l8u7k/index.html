The top 6 customer journey orchestration tools in 2026

Customer journey orchestration tools let developers define how events flow into multi-step workflows with delays, branches, and channel decisions. We compared six platforms on workflow depth, trigger flexibility, and the branching logic developers actually use in product notifications.

The short answer. Choose Courier if you want a visual builder with send-rate guardrails baked in. Choose SuprSend if you need multi-tenant workflows, batching, and a preference centre on one platform. Choose Knock for the cleanest developer experience and built-in multi-tenancy on every plan. Choose Novu if open-source control and self-hosting matter most. Choose Customer.io for data-driven journeys with a visual builder and unlimited push. Choose Braze when enterprise scale, predictive AI, and cross-channel orchestration justify a sales conversation.

At a glance

  • Courier: Best for: Visual builder with send-rate limits; Starting price: Free (10k sends/mo)

Courier

Courier is a notification platform that adds a visual journey builder on top of multi-channel delivery. Its workflows support event triggers, fixed and relative delays, and if/else branching on payload or profile data. Send limits let you cap global, per-user, or per-topic volume from the dashboard, which protects budgets and user experience without extra code. The Developer plan includes 10,000 sends a month with journeys, broadcasts, and the MCP server.

Pros: Visual workflow editor; granular send limits per topic. Cons: Multi-tenancy and advanced routing need Enterprise; per-send pricing above the free tier. Pricing: Free tier 10,000 sends/month; Business $0.005 per send. For more info, visit their pricing page.

SuprSend

SuprSend is notification infrastructure built for product, transactional, and marketing messages across every channel from one platform. Workflows run from events, API calls, or broadcasts and include 25 node types: delay, wait-until, batch, digest, throttle, branch (up to 10 paths), and fetch. Smart channel routing sends one channel at a time and stops on engagement. Multi-tenancy supports sub-tenants up to five levels with per-tenant vendors and preference inheritance (Enterprise or paid add-on). Batching, digests, user preferences, and objects require the Business plan ($250/mo billed annually).

Pros: Deep workflow nodes; multi-tenant data model; in-app inbox and preference centre. Cons: Batching, digests, and preferences gated to Business; multi-tenancy on Enterprise/add-on; smaller brand than Knock or Courier. Pricing: Free 10,000 notifications/month; Essentials $100/mo (50k); Business $250/mo (50k); Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Knock

Knock provides a notification engine with a code-first API and a visual workflow editor. Every plan includes unlimited workflows, channels, and built-in multi-tenancy. The Developer tier gives 10,000 messages a month with batching, delays, and SSO via Google. Rate limits scale across five tiers up to 1,000 requests per second, with batch deduplication limits per entity.

Pros: Best-in-class developer experience; multi-tenancy on free tier; generous rate limits. Cons: Starter jumps to $250/mo; translations and per-customer branding need Enterprise. Pricing: Free 10,000 messages/month; Starter $250/mo (50k); Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Novu

Novu is an open-source notification platform (~40k GitHub stars) with a cloud offering and self-hosted Enterprise option. Workflows support code-first definitions, delays up to 30 days on Team, digest windows up to 30 days, and throttle up to 7 days. The Free plan allows 10,000 workflow runs, 20 workflows, and two environments. Pro starts at $30/mo for 30,000+ runs and removes Novu branding Novu Pro.

Pros: Open source; self-host on Enterprise; low Pro entry price. Cons: Workflow and step caps on lower plans; delay/digest limits scale with plan; multi-tenancy only on Enterprise. Pricing: Free 10,000 runs/month; Pro from $30/mo; Team from $250/mo; Enterprise custom. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Customer.io

Customer.io combines a visual workflow builder with a customer data platform. Journeys trigger on events, segment changes, or API calls and support delays, time windows, A/B branches, and LLM actions (AI Agent beta). Message frequency limits apply per workflow or channel for email, push, SMS, WhatsApp, and webhooks. Push, in-app, and webhook sends are unlimited on all plans; email and SMS pricing is custom.

Pros: Unified data and journeys; visual builder with AI nodes; unlimited push and in-app. Cons: No public per-message price; SMS/WhatsApp custom; advanced Agent skills need Premium/Enterprise. Pricing: Custom per profile and email volume; Startup program gives a year free. For more info, visit their pricing page.

Braze

Braze is an enterprise customer engagement platform where Canvas flows orchestrate cross-channel campaigns at MAU scale. Journeys use action-based triggers, predictive AI, personalized paths, and frequency capping. API rate limits vary by contract; /users/track bursts to 3,000 requests per three seconds on data-point contracts. Pricing scales with monthly active users across four editions (Go, Select, Pro, Enterprise) with no public numbers.

Pros: Predictive AI and real-time decisioning; massive scale; full channel suite. Cons: No public pricing; contract-based rate limits; overkill for product notification workflows. Pricing: Custom, MAU-based across four editions. For more info, visit their pricing page.
